# University Workforce Protocol

DreamNet Agent University is the root workforce authority for Claim Factories.
It teaches domain knowledge, but employment authority comes from demonstrated
work.

## Progression

```text
enrollment
  -> coursework
  -> ToolGym practical
  -> Security Gauntlet
  -> signed, scoped credential
  -> factory probation
  -> receipted production history
  -> recertification or revocation
```

A credential binds:

- one agent identity,
- one worker role,
- one policy version,
- one or more factory or city scopes,
- competency receipt digests,
- issue and expiry times,
- active, suspended, revoked, or expired state.

The factory checks credentials at the start of every run. Graduation is not
permanent blanket access.

## Curriculum Feedback

Every challenged claim, citation failure, privacy leak, calibration miss, and
policy violation can generate:

- a new ToolGym fixture,
- a remedial course,
- a recertification requirement,
- a revoked credential,
- a new specialized worker role.

Specialized spikes may provide expert evidence. They cannot promote claims
unless they independently earn the required factory credential.
