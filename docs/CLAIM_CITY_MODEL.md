# Claim City Model

A Claim City is a governed domain economy, not a larger agent.

Examples include:

- Market Intelligence City,
- Defense Evidence City,
- Infrastructure Reliability City,
- Lead Intelligence City,
- Software Assurance City.

## City Institutions

A mature city may contain:

- producing Claim Factories,
- independent Verification Factories,
- a retraction office,
- a Proof Drop office,
- a worker and credential registry,
- a claim registry,
- policy and budget administration,
- inter-factory routing,
- public and restricted evidence districts.

The city validates that at least one producer and one verifier exist and that
all registered factories share the city's policy version.

## What Comes After Cities

The next useful boundary is a federation, not a super-factory. A federation:

- transports portable claim envelopes,
- resolves identifiers,
- negotiates schema and policy versions,
- routes cross-domain verification,
- preserves each city's authority over its own domain.

It does not create a universal truth score or silently promote a claim across
jurisdictions.
